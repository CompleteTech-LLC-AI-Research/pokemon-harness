## Disk incident during this session

A `git archive` extraction of trunk failed with `No space left on device`. This
is a host resource condition, not a repository problem: `/workspace` has 58 GB
free on a 309 GB filesystem. `/tmp` held ~300 MB across ~1167 stale entries, most
of them disposable mutation/verification trees left by earlier sessions. The
cleanup command was blocked by this host's `rm -rf` safety guard, so **no
cleanup was performed and nothing was deleted**. The audit was re-run under
`/workspace/poke-harness/.scratch/` instead, which is where it belongs.

Consequence for anyone else on this host: scratch trees should go under
`/workspace`, not `/tmp`. The `/tmp` tmpfs is far too small for a tree of this
size and will silently truncate extractions.

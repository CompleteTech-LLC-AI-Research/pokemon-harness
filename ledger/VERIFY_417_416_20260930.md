## A methodology error I made and corrected

My first #417 probe used a hand-rolled `Sup` whose `__exit__` tested
`isinstance(exc[0], AssertionError)`. `exc[0]` is the exception *class* and
`exc[1]` the instance, so that `__exit__` returned `False` and the class did
not suppress at all. That probe reported all three rows **live** and
contradicted the issue.

Two things caught it: executing the fixture under real CPython showed the
`Sup()` rows propagating `AssertionError` when the issue says they are
swallowed, and re-reading #417's body showed the correct `return True` contract.
Rewriting the probe from the issue's own fixture reproduced the issue's table
exactly. Recording this because the earlier version would have "disproved" a
correct issue, and the failure mode — a probe whose stand-in class does not do
what the issue's does — is the same one that produced the false greens noted
elsewhere in this ledger.
